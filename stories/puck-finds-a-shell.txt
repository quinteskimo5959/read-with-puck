Puck Finds a Shell

Puck the duck went to the sand. The sun was hot.

Puck digs in the sand. Dig, dig, dig!

What is that? It is a big pink shell!

Puck picks up the shell and sets it on a rock.

In the shell is a crab! Snap, snap!

Puck jumps back. The crab jumps back!

Then the crab grins. Puck grins. They are pals.

The crab and Puck sit in the sun and munch on sweet seeds.
